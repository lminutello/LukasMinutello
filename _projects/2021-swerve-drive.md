[PLACEHOLDER] Swerve drive design for the Sciborgs, the Bronx Science FRC robotics team (team 1155), in the 2020/21 season.

[PLACEHOLDER] Each swerve module has two mechanisms: a swivel mechanism that rotates the orientation of the wheel, and a powertrain that spins the wheel itself. That lets the robot drive in any direction while facing any way.
