[PLACEHOLDER] For the 2023/24 car I designed the tie rods and steering column using carbon fiber tubing with aluminum inserts to cut weight.

[PLACEHOLDER] The carbon tubes are bonded to the aluminum inserts, so the joint is the weak point. I developed a more rigorous adhesive bonding process and validated it with torsion and pull-out testing, which showed a 50% increase in bond shear strength.

[PLACEHOLDER] I also modeled the carbon fiber tie rod tubes in ANSYS ACP using the manufacturer's layup data and ran an Euler buckling analysis to check the tubes against the manufacturer's reported critical load.
