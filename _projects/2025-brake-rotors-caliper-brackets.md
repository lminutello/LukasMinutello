[PLACEHOLDER] Floating brake rotors for the 2025/26 Cornell Baja Racing car, designed for reduced weight, minimized brake drag, less warping potential, more reliable pad contact, and better serviceability and ease of assembly.

[PLACEHOLDER] I ran temperature testing on the legacy rotors to get raw temperature values and quantify the effects of brake drag, then used those temperature logs with braking load cases in a coupled structural-thermal FEA in ANSYS. The results informed material selection, air gap size, and other design parameters.
