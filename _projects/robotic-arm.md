[PLACEHOLDER] A 3D printed robotic hand with servo-driven fingers that mirrors a person's hand in real time.

[PLACEHOLDER] MediaPipe's hand tracking finds the coordinates of points on the hand from a webcam feed. The distances between those points determine how far each servo needs to turn, and Python sends the commands to an Arduino through Pyfirmata.
